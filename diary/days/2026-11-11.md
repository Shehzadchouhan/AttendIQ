## Problems Faced & Design Decisions

<!-- REVIEW: Sheh to confirm he can explain this -->

1. **Preventing Retroactive Attendance Modifications**: Unrestricted post-session edits create auditing vulnerabilities and disputes. I implemented 24-hour automatic session locking via Alembic migration `0005_parent_audit_lock.py`: sessions older than 24 hours lock automatically, and any modification requires administrator privileges along with a mandatory explanation recorded in `unlock_reason` and `unlocked_at`.
2. **Multi-Tenant Tutor Data Scoping**: In multi-tutor tuition centres, tutors must not see or alter batches or students created by other teachers. I added Alembic migration `0006_multi_tenant_tutors.py` and filtered queries in `backend/app/api/v1/batches.py` and `backend/app/api/v1/students.py` by `created_by`/`teacher_id` unless the requester holds the `admin` role.
3. **Design Token Standardization**: Different views previously had disparate inline CSS colors and margins. I consolidated them into CSS custom properties in `frontend/src/styles/design-tokens.css` and built 11 modular UI primitives in `frontend/src/components/ui/` with a centralized `ThemeContext` supporting dark and light modes.
